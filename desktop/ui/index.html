              Appearance

              App icon
              Changes the icon of the window, the taskbar and the tray. The desktop shortcut keeps the original.

              Privacy

              Downloads & start-up

              Off by default: films stream without downloading. If on, files are saved to Downloads/UaN, so they still open when the sender’s PC is off later.
              UaN keeps running in the tray when you close the window, so friends can watch and download what you sent them. Quit it from the tray icon.

              Offline mailbox

              Offline mailbox
              When a friend is offline, your messages wait here (encrypted) so you can turn your PC off. Files still come from the sender's PC.

              Backup

              A backup keeps your friend code, friends, groups and messages in one password-protected file. With it you can move UaN to a new PC, or get everything back after reinstalling Windows.

              Phone

              Phone
              Your phone gets your friends, groups and messages, and keeps working when this PC is off.

              About & updates

              When a new version is out, UaN shows it in the sidebar. One click downloads it and UaN restarts by itself, nothing to install.
