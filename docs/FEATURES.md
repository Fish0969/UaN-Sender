# Everything UaN can do

The full list, from chat to watch parties. Back to the [overview](../README.md).

## How it works

- **No account.** On first start you pick a name. Your identity is a key stored on your PC.
- **Friend code.** You add friends with a code (`UAN-…`). It is hidden by default (handy while
  streaming your screen): use **Copy friend code**, or **Show friend code** to see it. Send it to a
  friend however you like; they paste it in *Paste a friend code* and you accept.
- **Anywhere in the world.** PCs find each other by their key and connect directly through routers
  when they can, otherwise through free public relays. Everything is end-to-end encrypted.
- **Groups.** Click **+** next to *Groups*, name it, pick friends. Everyone in a group sees
  everything posted there.
- **Files.** Drag files into a chat or use the paperclip. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: your friends
  stream it from your PC. Films can be **watched inside UaN** without downloading, or downloaded.
- **Remembers where you stopped.** Close a film (or leave a watch party) and it continues from
  the same spot next time.
- **Watch together.** In a group, every video has a *Watch together* button. The invitation pops up as a card wherever
  your friend is in UaN (and keeps arriving until they join or say no, on all their devices); in the party window you
  see for each person whether it reached them. The PC with the fastest
  connection shares the film, it streams for everyone, starts when everyone has enough loaded, and
  pauses for everyone if someone falls behind. If the host leaves, someone else takes over.
  A chat and a **playlist** sit next to the film (the next film starts by itself).
  Everyone chooses for themselves: just stream it, or **download the whole film first** (smoothest;
  the party waits for you, and a copy is kept in `Downloads/UaN`).
- **Sound the player can't decode (AC3, DTS …).** The film still plays inside UaN: a silent VLC in the background decodes just
  the sound and UaN plays it level with the picture, also after you jump around, and in a watch party. If VLC is
  missing, UaN offers to install it with one click. If this doesn't work, the film opens in VLC's window instead.
- **Open in VLC.** If a film's picture can't play inside UaN, it opens in VLC by itself, streamed without
  downloading. *Open in VLC* does it anytime. In a watch party, *Watch in VLC* keeps VLC in
  sync with everyone (play, pause, seek both ways); close VLC and you're back in UaN's own player.
- **Delete messages.** Hover a message and click × (on the phone: press and hold): delete it just for you, or (your own)
  for everyone. Deleting a file for everyone also stops sharing it, stops friends' downloads of it and removes the
  copies their UaN fetched by itself, also if they were offline when you deleted it.
- **Channels.** Groups can have channels besides *general* (tabs above the messages; + makes a new one). A channel can be locked with a password: its
  messages are encrypted with that password, so only people who know it can read them. The password is asked every time
  you open the channel.
- **Groups.** Click a group's name (or right-click it in the list) for members, adding people and more.
  Anyone in the group can add their friends; new members see the earlier messages too. Clear a chat's
  messages for yourself, or (the group's creator) for everyone.
- **Messages.** Reply to a message (it is quoted above your answer), edit your own, pin important ones to
  the top of the chat, and write **bold**, *italic*, ~~strike~~ or `code`. In groups, @Name mentions someone.
- **Quiet when you want.** Mute a chat, group or channel (an @mention or an answer to you still comes through).
  Click your picture to be Online, Away or Do not disturb (no notifications at all).
- **Folders.** Send a whole folder (paperclip, *Send a folder…*, or drop it in): first you see which kinds of files are
  in it (.mkv, .srt, .jpg …) and tick the ones that should go. It shows up as one folder in the chat. Click it to see what is inside, with
  previews of photos and videos: download any file on its own, tick several (Shift-click ticks a range), or
  **Download folder** (everything lands in a folder of the same name in Downloads, sub-folders kept). Click a
  picture or film in the folder to open it big; the arrows (or the arrow keys) go through the others, and Esc
  comes back to the folder.
- **Photos sent together** show up as one grid, like in Discord. In groups, each message has the sender's picture beside it.
- **Backup.** Settings → Backup saves your friend code, friends, groups and messages in one password-protected
  file, to move UaN to a new PC or restore it after reinstalling.
- **Typing and seen.** "… is typing", and small profile pictures next to the time of the last message each person has
  seen, like in Messenger. Both can be turned off in Settings.
- **Previews.** Photos and videos show a preview picture (and the video's length) in the chat, even
  when the sender's PC is off.
- **Offline friends.** Messages, previews and small files (photos, short clips up to 24 MB) wait
  encrypted in a free mailbox, so they arrive even if you have turned your PC off by the time your
  friend comes online.
- **Automatic downloads** (off by default, turn on in Settings): files friends send are downloaded in
  the background to `Downloads/UaN`, so they still open later when the sender is offline.
- **Search** (Ctrl+F), a **gallery** of each chat's photos, videos and files, **subtitles**
  (send `film.srt` next to `film.mp4`, or **+ Subtitles** in the player; **CC** turns them on or off),
  **profile picture** (upload one or pick a ready-made one), **status**, **17 themes** (including a Christmas one with falling
  snow), and a choice of **app icons**. The *Media and files* button shows every photo and video of a chat as a grid.
- **Your own themes.** The **+** tile next to the themes takes an `.html` page, a video or a picture and puts it behind
  the chat. A page runs locked away from the app: it can draw and animate, but cannot reach UaN, your files or the
  internet, so it has to be one self-contained file (on Linux and Android pages are shown without scripts). A page can
  ask for light panels with `<meta name="uan-theme" content="light">` and an accent colour with
  `<meta name="uan-accent" content="#ff8800">`. A video's sound is off unless you turn it on (you're asked when adding
  it). Any picture, video or page someone sends can become your theme: right-click it in the chat, *Use as theme*.
- **Theme settings.** Double-click one of your themes, or right-click it → *Theme settings…*, and a panel opens next to
  Settings: **sound** on or off, **volume**, **blur** and **brightness**, each with a slider and a box to type the number.
  The background changes while you drag. *Reset* goes back to the defaults.
- **Settings in pages.** The pages are listed on the left (Profile, Appearance, Interface style, Privacy, Downloads &
  start-up, Offline mailbox, Backup, Phone, About & updates), the page itself on the right. The installed version is shown at the bottom.
- **Interface style.** Besides the colours of a theme you can change the shape and feel of the whole app: **Liquid glass**
  (frosted, translucent panels), **Pixel** (square corners, thick edges, hard shadows) or **Flat**. **Default** is the usual
  look and is what everyone starts with; pick it any time to go back.
- **Title bar and full screen (Windows).** UaN draws its own title bar in the colours of your theme. **F11** shows the app full screen and back.
- **Groups.** The owner can name **co-owners**. Owners and co-owners can rename the group, add and remove people, manage
  channels, change the group picture, clear the chat for everyone and delete anyone's message; only the owner deletes
  the group or names co-owners. The header shows who is online with small pictures (you count too; Settings turns
  the pictures off). Right-click a friend or group to **pin** it to the top (3 of each).
- **Small updates.** When a new version is out, a card in the sidebar says so (UaN checks when it starts and every few
  hours; *Settings → About & updates → Check for updates* does it on the spot). One click on *Update*: it downloads only the
  new program (a few MB) and switches over by itself, no installer, no uninstaller, everything stays. On Linux, the AppImage also only downloads the new program
  (a few MB), not the whole 150 MB package.

## Android

*The phone app is paused: no new versions for now (the last one is 1.9.2).*

Download `UaN_<version>_android.apk` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/Fish0969/UaN-Sender/releases)
on the phone and open it (Android asks once to allow installing apps from the browser). Updates install over
the old version, everything stays. The phone does everything the PC does except VLC, folders and the tray;
films stream to the phone, and the **Photos** button sends pictures and videos from the gallery. Android stops apps that are in the
background for a long time, so files you send from the phone are only available while UaN is open there.

**Use your PC's account on the phone.** On the PC: Settings → *Sign in on your phone*; it shows a short code. On the
phone: *Already use UaN on your PC? Sign in with it* (or Settings → Your PC) and type the code. The phone gets your
friends, groups and messages and becomes a second device of the same account: friends reach you on both, and
**the phone keeps working when the PC is off**. When both are on again they catch up with each other.

## Good to know

- **Big files need both PCs on at the same time once**: the sender's PC must be on, with UaN running,
  until the friend has watched or downloaded it (turn on automatic downloads in Settings if you
  want this to happen in the background). That's why UaN starts with your PC and stays in the tray when you close the window.
  Quit it from the tray icon menu.
- If two PCs can't connect directly, traffic goes through a public relay, which is much slower. The chat
  header shows which one you have ("direct connection" or "through a relay server"). Speed is also limited by
  the sender's upload speed.
- Some video formats (e.g. some AVI files) can't be played inside UaN; those open in VLC
  automatically (free, [videolan.org](https://www.videolan.org)).
